## What this whitepaper covers

- The Saudi health data portability problem and the international pilgrim gap
- Global regulatory context: EU EHDS (March 2025), WHO IHR amendments (September 2025), WHO GDHCN
- Three-module architecture: HealthChain Core, PalmaAI, Patient Wallet
- Canonical credential schema: W3C VC 2.0 + SD-JWT + FHIR R4
- HealthChain Core smart contract architecture and Fuji testnet deployment
- Hajj health credential verification pilot design
- International expansion strategy and issuer onboarding model
- Compliance: PDPL, SDAIA AI Ethics Principles, ISO 42001, EU EHDS
